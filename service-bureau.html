Common questions

Do I need multiple offices today?

No. The survey helps us understand your current experience, capacity and growth plan so we can tell whether the program fits.

What support is included?

Qualified partners receive onboarding, training, operational guidance and ongoing support for tax software and service bureau growth.

Can I operate under my own brand?

Yes. The program includes co-branding options so your preparers see your business, backed by SwiftFP.

How much can a service bureau earn?

It depends on how many offices you support, their volume and the fees you set. Use the revenue calculator in Resources for an estimate, then walk through your own numbers with our team.

What happens after the survey?

The team reviews your responses. If there is a potential fit, someone follows up to discuss goals, responsibilities and next steps.
