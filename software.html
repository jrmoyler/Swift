Common questions

Is SFP Works online or desktop software?

SFP Works is cloud-based. You work in the browser without maintaining a local installation, and tax updates apply automatically.

Can I use it with employees or multiple preparers?

Yes. Multi-user access is included in Premium, with privileges you set per preparer, such as e-file, bank, check printing and reports.

Which package should I choose?

Elite fits lower return volume: $399.99 with 30 returns included, then $10 per additional return. Premium offers unlimited filing. During a demo the team can compare both against your expected volume.

How does the $99 Premium option work?

You pay $99 today and $800 by February 15, 2027, for $899 total. Paying up front instead costs $674.25, a promotional price.

Do I need an EFIN and PTIN?

Tax professionals generally need the appropriate IRS credentials to prepare and e-file returns. SwiftFP offers free EFIN application guidance and can explain what you need.

Does SwiftFP provide training and support?

Yes. Onboarding, training, technical support and business-growth guidance are part of working with SwiftFP.
