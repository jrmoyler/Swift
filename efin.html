EFIN questions

What is an EFIN?

An Electronic Filing Identification Number, issued by the IRS to approved e-file providers. A tax office generally needs an active EFIN to transmit client returns through professional tax software.

Does SwiftFP issue or approve EFINs?

No. The IRS issues EFINs and makes every approval decision. SwiftFP provides independent guidance so you understand and complete the application.

Is the EFIN application free?

The IRS does not charge an application fee. Other costs may apply to supporting requirements or third-party services, depending on your situation.

How long does approval take?

It varies with the application, suitability review, fingerprinting and whether the IRS needs more information. Apply as early as you can before tax season.

What happens during my appointment?

We review your readiness, help you through the application, explain what information is required and map your next steps.
